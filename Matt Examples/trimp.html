Training week

Add every session file from one week, set the player and session type, and calculate them together. Each player is compared with the population guidelines of about 450–500 AU iTRIMP and about 30 (± 20) minutes above 90% HRmax per week (Buchheit et al., 2025). Match minutes are kept separate because match HR can overstate aerobic demand.

The ΔHR ratio

Every TRIMP based on heart rate reserve starts by expressing HR as a fraction of the range between rest and maximum.

ΔHR = (HR − HRrest) ÷ (HRmax − HRrest)

Macro and micro

iTRIMP works best as a macro measure of cumulative cardiovascular load across a week or block. Time above 90% HRmax works as a micro target you can prescribe within a session. Two sessions can produce the same TRIMP from very different intensity distributions, so read both together (Buchheit et al., 2025).

File formats

  • One column of HR. The sampling rate you choose sets the time step.
  • Time and HR columns, with or without a header. Time can be seconds, mm:ss or h:mm:ss.ms, and the real gap between samples is used.
  • Exports with information rows (Polar, CPET software). The tool searches for a header such as HR, HR (bpm) or Heart rate.
  • Values outside 30 to 240 bpm are treated as dropouts. Gaps longer than five times the usual interval are not counted as exercise time.

How A and B are fitted

The natural log of each lactate value is regressed on the ΔHR ratio by ordinary least squares. The slope is B and A = eintercept. This matches Excel's exponential trendline, so values should agree with a spreadsheet fit. HR at 2 and 4 mmol·L⁻¹ are found by straight-line interpolation between the observed stages either side of each value, not from the fitted curve.

Some TRIMP spreadsheets use 2.712 in place of e (2.718). That changes iTRIMP slightly, so values here can sit a little above a spreadsheet that uses 2.712.

Privacy and storage

Files are read by your browser and never uploaded. Players are stored in this browser's local storage, and clearing browser data erases them, so export a copy if you need to keep it. Sample sessions and the sample week are generated on the spot and are clearly labelled as illustrative.
