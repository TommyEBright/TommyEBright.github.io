Activity 1

Acute response or chronic adaptation?

Acute responses are immediate, temporary changes during and shortly after a single bout of exercise. Chronic adaptations develop over weeks to months of repeated training. Sort each statement below.

Score: 0 / 0

Key idea: repeated acute stress drives chronic adaptation. Each session sends a signal, and the signals add up over weeks of training. This is the overload principle: you manipulate frequency, intensity, time and type (FITT).
