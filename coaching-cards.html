        Quick rules

        Good coaching in the gym

        • Fewest words, biggest effect. Overcoaching gets in the way of the lifter feeling and fixing the movement.
        • Early on, external cues (about the bar, floor or a target) usually work well. Internal cues (about a body part) suit small refinements later.
        • Give feedback after the set. Lots of feedback at first, then less as the lifter improves.
        • Agree the spot before the set (e.g. how many reps, the liftoff count, the word for "take it").
