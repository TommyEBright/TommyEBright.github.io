Plan a t-test study. Exact calculations using the noncentral t distribution, the same method JASP's Power module and G*Power use. Lakens, chapter 8.

Predict first. To have 80% power to detect d = 0.5 with two independent groups (α = .05), how many participants do you need per group? Guess before you look.
Check your prediction
64 per group, so 128 in total. Many students guess 20–30. Halve the effect to d = 0.25 and you need about 4× as many (253 per group).
