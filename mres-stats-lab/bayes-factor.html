Bayesian t-test: prior, posterior and the Bayes factor

Reproduces JASP's default Bayesian t-test (Cauchy prior on effect size δ). Change the prior and see how the evidence changes. Lakens, chapters 3 and 4.

Predict first. In the JASP Directed Reading Activities data, the classical t-test gives p = .029. Is that strong evidence for an effect? Guess the Bayes factor BF10 before you load the preset.
Check your prediction
BF10 = 2.22: the data are only about twice as likely under H1 as under H0, which is anecdotal evidence. A p-value just under .05 often goes with weak relative evidence (Lakens 1.5 and 3.1). The directional hypothesis δ > 0, which the treatment was designed to test, gives BF+0 = 4.33.
