Stats Lab: from p-values to posteriors

Interactive companions to the Canvas learning resource. Work through them in order: each one starts with a prediction for you to make.

Stage 1 · Foundations

Which p-values should you expect?

Simulate thousands of studies. Type I error, power and Lindley's paradox.
Stage 2 · Estimation

The dance of the CIs

Coverage, capture percentage and how publication bias inflates effect sizes.
Stage 3 · Planning

Power and sensitivity

Exact sample size planning and the smallest effect your study can detect.
Stage 3 · Planning

Equivalence testing (TOST)

Evidence of absence, smallest effect sizes of interest, and the four possible conclusions.
Stage 4 · JASP analysis

Bayes factor explorer

Prior, posterior, Savage–Dickey ratio and a robustness check, matching JASP's output.
Stage 5 · Model checking

Residual detective

Diagnose regression problems from the same plots JASP shows, and feel how influence works.
Stage 6 · Visualisation

Show the data

Bar charts versus rainclouds, and Anscombe's quartet.
Stage 7 · Writing

Fix the results paragraph

Spot reporting errors in real JASP analyses, then compare with a model answer.
Stage 8 · Bayesian modelling

MCMC playground

Random walk, HMC and NUTS, live. Divergences, R̂, ESS and a computing-budget race.
Stage 8 · Bayesian modelling

LOO model comparison

PSIS-LOO, elpd differences and Pareto k̂, with draggable data.

R scripts for Stages 7–8

Download, open in RStudio and run line by line. Both scripts read the JASP Data Library files directly from JASP's GitHub repository.

  • stage7-jasp-to-r.R: the classical JASP analyses reproduced in R (t-tests, ANOVA, regression, TOST, Bayes factors, ggplot2).
  • stage8-brms.R: Bayesian regression in brms with priors, MCMC diagnostics, posterior predictive checks and LOO.

Built for MRes students as an open educational companion to Lakens, D. (2022) Improving Your Statistical Inferences (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Datasets: JASP Data Library. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is tracked or stored.
