# Cardiovascular Function and Testing (RSEC01)

Interactive 20-slide deck for the RSEC01 cardiovascular health and functional testing practical: naming the heart, blood flow, vessels and venous return, conduction and the cardiac cycle, ECG, control of heart rate, Q = SV × HR and Frank–Starling, the acute response to exercise, the Fick principle, training adaptations, blood pressure, orthostatic hypotension and vasovagal syncope, pre-test screening, a Korotkoff sound simulator, measurement errors, and BP / resting HR classification with a feedback script.

- Keys: → / Space next, ← back, S presenter view, O overview, F full screen, B black screen.
- `?handout` shows every slide with its speaker notes for printing or saving as PDF.
- Widgets live in `assets/w-heart.js`, `assets/w-bp.js` and `assets/w-extra.js`; the engine (`deck.js`, `presenter.html`) is shared with the energy systems deck.
- BP categories follow the ESH grades (optimal < 120/80). Diastolic is recorded at the disappearance of Korotkoff sounds (phase V).
