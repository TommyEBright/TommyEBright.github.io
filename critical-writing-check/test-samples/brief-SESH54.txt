Task description
You will undertake a needs analysis to determine the physical demands and performance determinants of a given sport and athlete. The needs analysis will give you, as the sport scientist, an understanding of the physiological qualities required for optimum performance in your identified sport and athlete. Your report should be approximately 1750 words.
Learning outcomes assessed
LO1: Critically evaluate the role of performance physiology in the assessment and development of athletic performance.
LO2: Apply theoretical knowledge of performance physiology to practical sport and exercise contexts.
LO3: Design, implement and interpret appropriate physiological assessments for a range of athletic populations.
Suggested structure
Introduction: Guide the reader through the importance of completing a physiological needs analysis and how it can be used to inform test selection.
Main body: Review existing peer-reviewed publications that have identified the physiological determinants of the sport. Once the components of performance have been established, use this to critically explore which physiological tests should be selected to assess the athlete. Support test selection with reliability and validity evidence and acknowledge relevant limitations.
