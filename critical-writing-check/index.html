Open in a new tab

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser using fixed, transparent rules. There is no AI, no server and nothing is sent anywhere. Your text and brief are kept only in this browser, and "Clear my data" removes them. Opening a PDF or Word file downloads a small open-source reader to read it on your device. The file itself never leaves your device.

You do not need a finished draft. Check a paragraph as soon as you have written it, fix what is useful, and move on.

What are you checking?

This tool matches patterns. It will flag some sentences that are fine and miss some problems. It never rewrites your text, gives no mark, and does not replace tutor feedback or Studiosity.
