Diet recall analyser

Build your own figures from a 24-hour recall. Enter meals by hand or paste an export, then download each figure and its caption for your portfolio.

Predict first. Before you enter anything, guess your athlete's carbohydrate and protein intake in g/kg of body mass. Which meal do you think carries the most protein? Note your guesses and compare them with the figures below.

1. The athlete and the day

Carbohydrate ranges from Thomas et al. (2016). Protein 1.2 to 2.0 g/kg a day (Thomas et al. 2016) and about 0.3 g/kg per meal (Jäger et al. 2017). Fat 20 to 35% of energy (Thomas et al. 2016).

Or paste an export

Paste CSV or spreadsheet rows with a header row. Columns are matched by name: meal (or group), time, food, energy or kcal, carbohydrate, protein, fat, fibre. Cronometer's free export and most copied MyFitnessPal tables work.

    Method limits to discuss in your portfolio. A single 24-hour recall may not represent habitual intake, under-reporting is common in athletes, and app food databases include user-entered items that can be wrong (Capling et al. 2017). Say how you reduced these errors, for example multiple-pass prompts, portion-size aids and checking database entries.

    Energy from macronutrients uses 4 kcal/g carbohydrate and protein and 9 kcal/g fat. Alcohol is not included. Targets are population guidance; interpret them against the athlete's goals, sport and training phase.
