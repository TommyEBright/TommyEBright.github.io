SESH58

Nutrition Lab: assessment portfolio tools

Interactive companions to the Nutrition for Adaptation Canvas page. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

Data collection station

Diet recall analyser

Enter or paste your 24-hour recall and build your own labelled figures: g/kg targets, protein per meal and energy across the day. Download them as images.
Evaluation

Energy availability and fuelling

Calculate energy availability and match carbohydrate and protein targets to the training load of the day.
Reflection station

Reflective writing scaffold

Choose a model, write stage by stage and see whether your reflection is mostly description or moves into analysis and action.
Portfolio guidance

Portfolio planner and checker

Plan the five portfolio sections and run rule-based checks on your draft: voice, figure labels, citations and readability.
Feedback section

Athlete feedback builder

Turn your three key findings into a clear, practical one-page summary for your athlete.
Self-check

Nutrition for adaptation quiz

Nine applied scenarios on fuelling, protein, timing, energy availability and dietary assessment.

The targets in these tools come from published position statements (Thomas et al. 2016; Jäger et al. 2017; Kerksick et al. 2017; Mountjoy et al. 2023). They are starting points for discussion, not prescriptions. SESH58, Plymouth Marjon University.
