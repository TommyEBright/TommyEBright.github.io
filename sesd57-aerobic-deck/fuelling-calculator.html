Questions to answer in pairs

  1. How does the plan change for a 55 kg runner finishing in 4 h 30? Explain which parts change and why.
  2. A 75 kg cyclist expects to ride for 1 h 40 and wants to take 90 g per hour because "more must be better". Apply the guidelines to explain what you would advise instead.
  3. Use the food builder to reach about 60 g per hour for a runner who has not done any gut training. Describe two practical problems they might have on race day and how they could practise in training.
