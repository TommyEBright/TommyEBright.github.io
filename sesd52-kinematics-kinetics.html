1. What are we measuring?

About 5 minutes

Last year you measured movement with video and force platforms. Before we use both again, get the three key words straight.

Biomechanics

The study of how living things move, using the methods of mechanics (Hatze, 1974). In sport, it asks two questions: what happened, and why did it happen?

Kinematics

Describes motion without looking at what caused it: what happened.

  • position and displacement
  • velocity and acceleration
  • angles and angular velocity
  • timing (e.g. contact time, flight time)

Kinetics

Explains motion through the forces that cause it: why it happened.

  • force (e.g. ground reaction force)
  • impulse and momentum
  • moments of force (torque)
  • work, energy and power

The link: Newton’s second law, F = ma. Force (kinetics) causes acceleration (kinematics). Today you will see one jump through both lenses and watch the force turn into the movement.
