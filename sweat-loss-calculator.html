Use nude, towel-dried mass before and after exercise, with the bladder emptied where possible. Mass loss above the flag threshold is highlighted.

Formulas used

Mass loss (kg) = pre mass − post mass

Mass loss (%) = mass loss ÷ pre mass × 100

Sweat loss (l) = mass loss + fluid intake − urine output

Sweat rate (l per h) = sweat loss ÷ duration (h), and (ml per min) = sweat loss × 1000 ÷ duration (min)

Volume during exercise = lower of sweat rate and the maximum drinking rate, × duration. This assumes drinking to thirst, capped for safety.

Volume post-exercise = sweat loss × replacement %, still guided by thirst.

CHO (g per h) = drinking rate (ml per h) × drink concentration (%) ÷ 100
