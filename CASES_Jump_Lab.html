Why do we jump higher with a countermovement?

You have just seen two jumps on the force plate. This lab lets you look inside both of them, second by second, and work out why one goes higher than the other.

SJSquat jump

Lower into a squat, hold still for a moment, then push up as hard as you can. No dipping down first.

CMJCountermovement jump

Start standing tall, drop down quickly, then reverse straight away and push up as hard as you can.

What does a force plate measure? How hard you push down on the ground, measured 1000 times every second. The ground pushes back up with the same force. From that one signal we can work out your body weight, how fast you are moving, how far you have moved and how high you jump.
