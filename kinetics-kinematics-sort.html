Kinetics or kinematics?

Sort the cards as a group. Talk about each one before you place it.

Kinetics

The forces that cause movement. If you need force or mass to work it out, it is kinetics.

Kinematics

A description of movement. Position, distance, time, speed and angles, with no mention of force.

How to play. Drag each card into a column (or tap a card, then tap a column). When they are all placed, check your answers. Then show the answers to see why, and how you would measure each one in the lab.
