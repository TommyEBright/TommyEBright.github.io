Name that plane

The model will perform 10 mystery movements. For each one, pick the plane it moves in and the axis it rotates about.

  • 50 points for each correct plane or axis
  • Streak bonus for getting both right in a row
  • Speed bonus if you lock in within 10 seconds

Tip: use the pin test. Imagine a pin through the joint that the segment spins around. That pin is your axis, and the plane is at right angles to it.
