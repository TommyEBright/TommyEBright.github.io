Practical session | Supports the Practical Assessment (40%)

Step 1

Find the phases

  1. Open the worked example (a 180° pivot) to see the level of detail to aim for.
  2. Split your movement into four phases and name each one by editing the italic labels in the top row.
  3. Add one to three still frames per phase. Click a box to upload, drag an image in, or click the box and paste a screenshot (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V).
  4. Fill in each row with one point per line. Each line becomes a bullet point in the Word document.

Source: Science of Multidirectional Speed. Each phase has clear start and end events, one main aim, specific joint actions, named muscle groups with contraction type, and linked movement principles.
